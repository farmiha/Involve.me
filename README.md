# Involve.me

Find people to go with, with people you already go with. #doubledates made easy

**Target audience:** NYC adults ages 18–29 looking for friendship, networking, or nightlife — especially students, recent movers, and event-goers into concerts, sports, food, and culture.

## What This Is

Involve.me is a social app that helps people find others to actually go do things with. You can join as a solo person, a couple, a small group, or a bigger crew, and get matched with people based on location, interests, event type, timing, and group size.

The question we're trying to answer isn't "what's happening" or "who would I match with" — it's just: **who's actually coming with me?**

## The Problem

People skip out on things they want to do because they don't have anyone to go with. Friends are busy, live far away, or aren't into it. Since COVID, people are also just more hesitant to meet someone new on their own. So instead of going out, they stay home — that's the gap we're trying to close.

## How It Works

- **People First** – browse individuals and groups, figure out the plan later
- **Plan First** – already have the plan (concert, rave, dinner, game), find people to fill it
- **Crews** – group-to-group matching, not just person-to-person (this is where double dates fit)
- **Dare Crew** – spin a wheel, get matched with a person/group, both sides have 24 hours to accept before a crew chat opens

## Business Model

Free to use (18+), with optional paid features like crew boosts, TagAlong+, partner-funded rewards, and event insights. Dare Points reward participation and can be used for perks like Dare Passes, rerolls, and badges — not cashed out directly.

## Why Now

Post-COVID, people are more hesitant to leave their comfort zones, and loneliness rates back that up — the U.S. Surgeon General's 2023 advisory found that about half of U.S. adults experience loneliness. At the same time, people are a lot more comfortable meeting others through an app than they used to be.

## Status

Early-stage class project. Right now we're validating whether people would actually use this through interviews, surveys, and a manual (Wizard-of-Oz) matching pilot before building out full automated matching.
